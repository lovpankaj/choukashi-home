## Editorial rules

- Original message wording is retained, except marked customer-name and contact-number redactions. The reader explicitly marks omitted intervening messages.
- English explanations of native-script messages are labelled as explanations, not original quotes.
- Each example separates the customer’s situation, the benefit for the business, the observed outcome and its limits.
- A recorded appointment establishes booking, not attendance or purchase. A question sent to staff does not establish an answer or completed callback.
- Certain secondary examples show useful language handling or boundaries rather than commercial success. Their explanations retain those distinctions.
- Failed and suppressed outgoing drafts are excluded. Current product screenshots and their demo/report provenance are documented in `docs/product-screenshots.md`.

The owner confirmed that product language support extends across Indian languages in native scripts, Roman characters and mixed-script messages. The historical showcase is Hindi/Marathi/Hinglish; the language section explicitly distinguishes that sample from the product’s broader support.

The pooled 13–22 October metrics are separately documented in `docs/production-proof.md`. Public results contain no business-level breakdown. This is a dated snapshot, not a live counter.

The home page adds no trackers, analytics, forms, browser storage or external runtime font calls. Existing legal/support routes and brand assets are retained.
